Under the hood
What the model looks at
Form and class
It reads each runner's recent starts, beaten margins, class moves and time since its last run, weighed against thousands of past results.
Barrier, trip and going
The draw, the distance and the track condition all change how a race is run. The model accounts for each, and for how a given horse handles them.
Jockey and trainer
Strike rates for the people on the horse: overall, at the track, over the distance, and as a jockey and trainer pairing.
The market, and its moves
It sets its own price for each runner, compares it to the live market, and watches which way the money is going.
Calibrated, and checked
Every pick carries a probability that's measured against real results. When the model says 30%, those picks win about 30% of the time.
Re-fit on every result
As races are settled the model re-trains, so today's ratings reflect the latest form rather than a version from weeks ago.
Methodology
How Our AI Works

Every FunkyIQ model is built on the same principle: no single number tells the whole story, so we don't lean on one. Each model combines dozens of independent signals per outcome, and weights each one by how much it has actually predicted past results, not by hunch. As new results come in, those weights are recalibrated, so the model making today's calls is a slightly sharper version of yesterday's.

Model versus market

A prediction is only interesting if it disagrees with the crowd. For every selection, the model calculates its own implied probability and compares it to the probability baked into the current market price. When the two line up, there's no edge to act on. When the model rates an outcome materially higher than the market does, that gap — what we call the Edge — is surfaced explicitly, so you can see not just what the model likes, but where it thinks the market is wrong.

Calibrated confidence

A 70% prediction should be right about 70% of the time. That property — calibration — is what separates a probability you can act on from a number that merely looks confident. FunkyIQ grades every prediction with a calibrated confidence score and sorts outcomes into clear tiers, so you always know how much conviction sits behind a pick and when it's worth backing.

Always learning

The engines never stop running. Behind the scenes they continuously ingest fresh results, re-fit their patterns, and re-check their own calibration. The predictions you see reflect everything the system learned from the last outcome — not a snapshot frozen weeks ago.

Reference
Key Terms, Explained
Win Probability
The model's estimate of how likely an outcome is, expressed as a percentage. A field's win probabilities always sum to 100%.
Calibration
The quality that makes a probability trustworthy: over many predictions, outcomes rated 40% should happen about 40% of the time.
Market-Implied Probability
The chance a price implies, calculated as 1 divided by the decimal odds. A $4 runner implies a 25% chance.
Edge (Overlay)
The gap between the model's probability and the market-implied probability. A positive edge means the model rates the outcome higher than the price does.
Place
A finish in the paying positions — typically the top three in larger fields, top two in smaller ones. A place bet wins if the runner places.
Starting Price (SP)
The odds on a runner at the moment the race jumps — the standard reference price for settling and comparison.
Barrier Draw
The starting gate a horse is allocated. Inside and outside draws can materially help or hurt depending on distance and track.
Track Condition (Going)
The state of the racing surface, rated from Firm through Good, Soft and Heavy. It changes how a race is likely to be run.
Quinella
An exotic bet requiring you to pick the first two runners home in either order.
Trifecta
An exotic bet requiring the first three runners home in the correct order.
First Four
An exotic bet requiring the first four runners home in the correct order.
Head-to-Head
In footy, the market on which of the two teams wins the match outright — the simplest form of the bet.
Confidence Tier
FunkyIQ's grade for how much conviction sits behind a top pick, from Strong up to Premium.
